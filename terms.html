2. What Layaa does, and what it does not

In plain words: Layaa looks for apps that show pop-up ads and tells you about them. It is a useful check, not a guarantee. No security product catches everything.

Layaa:

  • checks the installed apps on a phone about every two days;
  • looks at where each app came from, what permissions it holds, whether it hides itself, and how much it runs in the background;
  • warns when an app it considers dangerous is installed;
  • tells you which app appears to be the problem and offers to help remove it.

Layaa does not:

  • block an app from being installed — Android does not permit an ordinary app to do that, so Layaa warns immediately afterwards instead;
  • remove any app by itself — Android always asks the person holding the phone to confirm;
  • read messages, photos, documents, or what is on the screen;
  • claim to detect every harmful app. New ones appear constantly, and some will be missed.

Layaa may also flag an app that turns out to be harmless. You decide whether to remove anything. If you believe we have got it wrong, please tell us at hello@layaa.io — see section 10.
