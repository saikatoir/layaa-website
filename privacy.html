Layaa is a security app, so it has to look at what is installed on a phone. This page says exactly what that means — and, just as importantly, what we never look at.

1. Who we are

Layaa is operated by Nurul Huda Soikottulla Laden trading as Layaa ABN 38 505 938 270, of Kent Street, Niagara Park NSW 2250, Australia. We are bound by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

Privacy enquiries: privacy@layaa.com.au

2. The two people involved

Layaa is usually set up by a family member — the carer — on the phone of the person being protected (the protected person). We hold different information about each.

3. What we collect

From the protected phone

WhatWhy
The list of installed apps — app name, package name, signing certificate, where it was installed from, the permissions it asks for, and when it was installed This is the check. Without it there is nothing to look at.
How apps behave — how long each runs in the background, how often it opens itself, how many notifications it sends Finds apps that show ads or run constantly

What we concluded about each app, and whether you removed or kept it Improves detection and reduces false alarms

From the carer

Your email address, and whether your subscription is active. Nothing else.

4. What we never collect

In plain words: Layaa can see which apps are on the phone and how busy they are. It cannot see what you do in them.

We do not collect:

  • Any content. Not messages, emails, photos, documents, or anything typed. Layaa records that an app ran — never what was on screen.
  • Browsing history or web activity.
  • Location.
  • Contacts, call logs, microphone or camera.
  • Payment card details. See section 6.

Layaa also does not use Android’s accessibility service, which can read the screen. The app is built deliberately without it.

5. Consent from the protected person

The carer sets Layaa up, but the phone belongs to the protected person. During setup, their phone shows what Layaa will collect and asks the person holding it to confirm both that it is their phone and that their family may receive alerts about it. Layaa does not begin checking until that confirmation is given.

The protected person can withdraw consent at any time by uninstalling Layaa or by contacting us.

6. Payments

In plain words: Google handles all the money. We never see your card.

All subscriptions are purchased and processed through Google Play, with Google as the seller. Your payment details are given to Google, never to us. We receive only a record of whether a subscription is active, and Google pays us our share separately.

Google’s handling of your payment information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Where your information is kept

  • Stored in Australia, in Sydney, with our hosting provider Supabase.
  • Encrypted while travelling over the internet and while stored.
  • Records of individual app activity are deleted after 90 days.
  • On the protected phone, the account password is never stored. It is exchanged at sign-in for a limited device key that can report check results and nothing else — it cannot reach billing or account settings.

8. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information, and we never use it for advertising.

We share it only with:

  • our hosting provider, acting on our instructions;
  • Google, for processing your subscription;
  • law enforcement or regulators, where the law requires it.

We use anonymous, combined statistics — for example, how common a particular app is across all Layaa phones — to improve detection. These cannot be traced back to any person.

9. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act you may ask us to give you access to your personal information, correct it, or delete it. Write to privacy@layaa.com.au and we will respond within 30 days. There is no charge.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and everything we hold at any time — in the app, or from our account deletion page. Deletion removes the household, every device record and all check history within 30 days, and from backups within a further 90 days.

Deleting your account does not cancel your Google Play subscription. Cancel that separately in the Google Play app — see our Terms.

10. Data breaches

If a breach occurs that is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify the people affected and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, as the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires.

11. Children

Layaa is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

12. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, write to privacy@layaa.com.au. We will acknowledge within 2 business days and respond within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

13. Changes

We will post any change on this page. If a change materially affects you, we will tell the carer in the app before it takes effect.
